# Time Warp Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026

### 1. Overview

Time Warp is a time utility for people who collect video from DVR and CCTV systems. It has a live clock, a Time Calculator (Time Difference, Corrected Time and Retention Date) and Daylight Saving help. This policy explains how Time Warp handles data.

**In short: Time Warp does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.**

### 2. What Time Warp Stores

Time Warp keeps a few settings and values on your device only, using iOS's standard app storage:

- Whether **Daylight Saving Help** is turned on or off
- Your most recent calculator results (for example a time difference or retention date), so they are still there when you come back to the app

These stay on your device. They are not sent anywhere, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

### 3. What Time Warp Does Not Do

Time Warp does **not**:
- Require an account or sign-in
- Connect to the internet or any server
- Collect analytics, usage data or crash data of its own
- Access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
- Use cookies, tracking or advertising identifiers
- Share or sell data to anyone

### 4. Time and Time Zone

The clock and calculator use your device's own time and time zone. Daylight Saving dates come from iOS's built-in time zone information, on the device. Nothing is looked up online.

### 5. Apple Services

If you install Time Warp through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may share crash reports or feedback you choose to send with the developer, according to Apple's own privacy policy and your device settings. Time Warp itself does not collect this information.

### 6. Children's Privacy

Time Warp is designed for professional use and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

### 7. Your Rights

Because Time Warp does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything Time Warp stores by deleting the app.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date.
