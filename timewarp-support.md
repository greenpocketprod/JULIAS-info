### What Time Warp Does

- **Clock:** a live clock for comparing against a DVR's on-screen time.
- **Time Difference:** compare the DVR's time to the real time to find how fast or slow it is.
- **Corrected Time:** enter the real time you need and get the matching DVR time to download.
- **Retention Date:** work out how far back a system's footage goes.
- **Daylight Saving Help:** warns when footage is from before a clock change and helps adjust for DVRs that don't change their own clocks.

### Common Questions

#### The Daylight Saving card keeps appearing
It shows when the footage you need is from before a clock change. If you work somewhere the clocks never change, or don't want it, turn it off in **Settings → Daylight Saving Help**.

#### The Daylight Saving dates look wrong
Time Warp uses your phone's time zone. Check **Settings → General → Date & Time** on your iPhone and make sure the time zone is correct.

#### My last calculation is still there
Time Warp remembers your last results so they're there when you come back. Use **Reset Values** on a calculator screen, or **Reset All Values** on the Time Calculator menu, to clear them.

#### Which version am I using?
Open **Settings** in Time Warp. The version and build number are under **About**.
